Simple P2P Audio Streaming

Instructions:
  1. Sender: Click "Start Microphone" → "Create Offer" → Copy the offer
  2. Receiver: Paste the offer → "Set Offer & Create Answer" → Copy the answer
  3. Sender: Paste the answer → "Set Answer"
  4. Audio should now be streaming!
